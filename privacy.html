Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Dawnbloom is built to be calm and transparent. Your focus history stays local, and we collect only the limited information needed to operate purchases, understand the product, and fix errors.

Local focus data

  • Your sky light, streak, freeze days, selected theme, focus-session state, wallpaper status, and rotating intention are stored on your device.
  • This data is not included in cloud backup and does not transfer to a new phone.
  • You can remove it with Reset Dawnbloom data in Settings or by clearing the app's storage.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, sale of data, location access, or contacts access.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.

Wallpaper and sharing

  • Your sky and share card are generated on your device.
  • Android receives the sky only when you ask to set the wallpaper.
  • If you share a card, the app you choose through Android's share sheet controls what happens next.

Purchases

  • Google Play processes payments. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous app-user identifier, purchase history, and entitlement status so Dawnbloom can unlock Plus and restore purchases.
  • Femi Studio LC does not receive your card details.
  • See the Google Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Product analytics and error reports

  • When anonymous diagnostics are enabled, Dawnbloom sends a random installation identifier, app interactions such as wallpaper set or focus session completed, purchase events, and scrubbed error diagnostics over HTTPS.
  • These events do not include your intention text, advertising ID, contacts, location, or focus-history contents.
  • The identifier is used only to understand Dawnbloom and diagnose errors, not to follow you across apps.

Your choices

  • Turn off Share anonymous diagnostics in Settings to stop new product analytics and error reports.
  • Copy your privacy ID from Settings and email legal@femi.studio to request deletion of analytics linked to that identifier.
  • Resetting app data creates a new random identifier, but does not by itself identify older anonymous events for deletion.

Children

  • Dawnbloom is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the in-app policy.
