Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Dawnbloom is built to be calm and transparent, and that includes how it treats your data. The short version: we don't have accounts, we don't run servers for your data, and we don't track you.

What we store

  • Everything Dawnbloom needs (your sky's light, your streak and freeze days, your chosen theme, and the rotating intention) is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your phone except when you choose to share.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no selling of data.
  • No background location or contacts access.

Wallpaper

  • When you tap "Set as my lock screen", the sky image is generated on your device and handed to Android's system wallpaper service. The image stays on your device.

Sharing

  • Your share card is created on your device. If you share it, Android's share sheet sends the image to the app you pick. What happens next is governed by that app.

Purchases (Dawnbloom Plus)

  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. To do that, a pseudonymous app-user identifier and your purchase and entitlement details are processed by RevenueCat and Google. We never receive your card details. See Google Play's and RevenueCat's privacy policies for their handling.

Analytics & crashes

  • Dawnbloom records anonymous, non-identifying product events and crash diagnostics to help us fix bugs and understand which features help. They contain no personal content and no tracking identifiers.

Children

  • Dawnbloom is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the in-app copy.
